Privacy Policy

Attested Uploads accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels. Data is used solely to upload and manage videos and read channel analytics. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

OAuth tokens are stored privately on systems controlled by the owner and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Attested Uploads complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
